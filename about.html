About Us

Our story, our passion, and our commitment to quality

Our Story

Blue Cliff Revival was founded by Thomas Samatoy with a simple mission: to bring new life to cherished leather items through craftsmanship and quality materials.

Thomas's journey into leather repair began over 15 years ago when he restored his first leather couch. What started as a hobby and personal interest quickly developed into a passion for preserving and extending the life of quality leather goods.

After years of working in the corporate world, Thomas decided to pursue his passion full-time and founded Blue Cliff Revival in 2018. Drawing on his meticulous attention to detail and commitment to quality, he built a reputation for exceptional craftsmanship and customer service in Northern Virginia.

"I believe that quality leather items tell a story and deserve to be preserved," says Thomas. "There's something deeply satisfying about taking a damaged piece that someone cares about and restoring it to its former glory - or even making it better than it was before."

Our Approach

Quality Materials

We use only premium, professional-grade materials specifically formulated for leather repair and restoration. This ensures durability and the most natural-looking results.

Expert Craftsmanship

Our team combines traditional techniques with modern innovations to deliver superior repairs that stand the test of time.

Convenience

We provide mobile services throughout Northern Virginia, coming to your home or business to perform repairs with minimal disruption.

Customer Satisfaction

We're not satisfied until you're delighted with the results. We stand behind our work and are committed to your complete satisfaction.

Our Service Area

Blue Cliff Revival proudly serves the Northern Virginia area, including Woodbridge, Lake Ridge, Dale City, Dumfries, Manassas, Springfield, Fairfax, Alexandria, and surrounding communities.

We offer both in-shop repairs at our Woodbridge location and mobile services where we come to you.
