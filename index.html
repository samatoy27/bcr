About Us

Quality Leather Repair

Blue Cliff Revival was founded with a passion for restoring leather goods to their former glory. We specialize in leather repair and upholstery, including vinyls, car seats, couches, chairs, and more.

We take pride in using only the highest quality materials and techniques to ensure your leather items look as good as new. Our service can be provided in your home, business, or at our workshop depending on the nature of the repair.

Whether it's that leather recliner you've had forever, your car seat, or your uncle's favorite leather jacket, we treat every project with the care and respect it deserves.
